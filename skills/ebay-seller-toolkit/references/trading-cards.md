# Trading Card Analysis & eBay Listing Reference

## Card Analysis Workflow

### Step 1: Identify the Card

Gather the following information:
- **Set/Year**: e.g., 2023 Topps Chrome
- **Player/Character**: e.g., Ja Morant, Charizard
- **Card Number**: Found on the back
- **Variant**: Base, refractor, auto, patch, numbered (/25, /50, /99)
- **Condition**: Raw or graded (PSA, BGS, SGC)

### Step 2: Assess Condition (Raw Cards)

| Grade Estimate | Characteristics |
|---------------|-----------------|
| Gem Mint (PSA 10 / BGS 9.5+) | Perfect centering, sharp corners, no surface issues, no print defects |
| Mint (PSA 9 / BGS 9) | Near-perfect, minor centering variance, one tiny flaw allowed |
| Near Mint-Mint (PSA 8) | Slight corner wear, minor centering issues, light surface marks |
| Near Mint (PSA 7) | Visible corner wear, off-center, light scratches |
| Excellent (PSA 5-6) | Noticeable wear, soft corners, surface issues |
| Below EX | Significant wear — typically not worth grading |

### Step 3: Research Market Value

1. Search eBay sold listings with exact card description
2. Filter by condition (raw vs. graded, specific grade)
3. Note recent sale prices (last 30-90 days)
4. Check alternative platforms: COMC, PWCC, MySlabs
5. Consider population reports for graded cards (lower pop = higher premium)

## Grading ROI Calculator

### Decision Framework

```
Grading ROI = (Expected Graded Value - Raw Value - Grading Cost - Shipping) / (Raw Value + Grading Cost + Shipping) × 100%
```

### Grading Cost Comparison

| Service | Standard Turnaround | Cost | Best For |
|---------|-------------------|------|----------|
| PSA | 65 business days | $25/card | Highest resale value, most recognized |
| BGS (Beckett) | 45 business days | $30/card | Pristine sub-grades command premium |
| SGC | 30 business days | $20/card | Fastest, competitive pricing, gaining market share |
| CGC | 50 business days | $20/card | Newer service, growing acceptance |

### When to Grade

- Card raw value is >$50 AND expected graded value is >2x raw value
- Card has strong chance of PSA 9 or 10 (population matters)
- Player/character is trending upward
- Card is a key rookie, iconic design, or low print run

### When NOT to Grade

- Card has visible defects (likely PSA 7 or below)
- Raw and graded prices are similar (common base cards)
- Card value is under $20 raw (grading cost erases margin)
- Player/character is declining in popularity

## eBay Listing Optimization for Cards

### Title Format

```
[Year] [Set Name] [Variant] #[Number] [Player] [Grade if graded] [Key Feature]
```

**Examples:**
```
2023 Topps Chrome Refractor #150 Ja Morant PSA 10 Gem Mint Memphis Grizzlies
2024 Pokemon Obsidian Flames #197 Charizard ex SAR NM Raw Japanese
```

### Photography for Cards

1. Use a clean, plain background (black or white)
2. Photograph front and back
3. Show close-ups of corners and centering
4. For graded cards, photograph the slab front, back, and label
5. Use natural or diffused light — avoid glare
6. Include a ruler or penny for scale if card size is unusual

### Item Specifics for Cards

Always fill in:
- Sport / Game / Franchise
- Player / Character Name
- Year Manufactured
- Set
- Card Number
- Graded / Ungraded
- Grading Company & Grade (if applicable)
- Autographed (Yes/No)
- Card Attributes (Refractor, Patch, Numbered, etc.)

## Market Intelligence

### Seasonal Trends

| Period | Trend |
|--------|-------|
| NFL Draft / NBA Draft | Rookie cards spike in anticipation |
| Playoffs / Championships | Active player cards rise with performance |
| Off-season | Generally lower demand, good buying opportunity |
| Holiday season (Q4) | Gift buying increases volume |
| National Card Show | Increased awareness and demand |

### Price Impact Factors

- **Player performance**: Great games = short-term price spikes
- **Injuries**: Can cause rapid price drops
- **Trades**: Team changes create volatility
- **Rookie status**: First-year cards typically have highest premium
- **Population reports**: Lower PSA 10 population = higher premium
- **Social media hype**: Viral moments cause temporary spikes — sell into hype

### Cross-Platform Price Comparison

| Platform | Strengths | Fees |
|----------|-----------|------|
| eBay | Largest audience, highest liquidity | ~13% + payment processing |
| COMC | Low effort (consignment), large community | 5% fee + $0.50-$2.50 per card storage |
| PWCC | Premium auction for high-end cards | 0-10% seller fee depending on plan |
| MySlabs | Growing marketplace, competitive fees | ~8% seller fee |
| Facebook Groups | Zero fees, direct negotiation | 0% (but no buyer protection) |
| Card shows | Face-to-face, instant cash | Table cost + travel |

## Bulk Listing Tips

- Group similar cards into lots for efficiency
- Use eBay's bulk listing tool or third-party tools (e.g., List Perfectly, SellBrite)
- Create templates for common card types
- Pre-photograph in batches using consistent setup
- Price competitively for volume — lower margin but faster turnover
