# eBay Shipping Strategy Reference

## Shipping Options Comparison

| Method | Best For | Conversion Impact |
|--------|----------|-------------------|
| Free Shipping | Items under 5 lbs, clothing, small electronics | Highest — appears in "Free Shipping" filter |
| Calculated Shipping | Heavy/oversized items, furniture, equipment | Medium — buyers appreciate accurate cost |
| Flat Rate Shipping | When actual cost varies little by location | Medium — predictable for buyers |

## Free Shipping Strategy

- Factor shipping cost into the item price
- Works best when shipping is under $10
- Required for eBay "Top Rated Plus" badge (along with 1-day handling and 30-day returns)
- Items with free shipping rank higher in search results
- Calculate: `Listing Price = Target Sale Price + Average Shipping Cost`

## Calculated Shipping Strategy

- Best for items over 5 lbs or with irregular dimensions
- eBay calculates cost based on buyer's location
- Requires accurate package weight and dimensions
- Always add packaging material weight (0.5-1 lb buffer)
- Tip: Enter dimensions of the packed box, not the item itself

## Carrier Comparison (US Domestic)

| Carrier | Best For | Notes |
|---------|----------|-------|
| USPS First Class | Items under 1 lb | Cheapest for lightweight |
| USPS Priority Mail | 1-5 lb items | Free boxes, 2-3 day delivery |
| USPS Priority Flat Rate | Dense/heavy small items | Price doesn't change by weight |
| UPS Ground | 5-50 lb items | Good for medium packages |
| FedEx Ground | 5-150 lb items | Often cheapest for heavy items |
| FedEx Freight / UPS Freight | Items over 150 lbs | Required for oversized items |

## Packaging Best Practices

1. **Measure and weigh BEFORE listing** — incorrect dimensions cause losses
2. **Use appropriate box size** — oversized boxes increase dimensional weight charges
3. **Double-box fragile items** — inner box + outer box with 2" packing material between
4. **Waterproof vulnerable items** — use poly bags inside box for electronics
5. **Save packaging materials** — reuse boxes, bubble wrap, and packing peanuts

## Handling Time

- **1-day handling** recommended for Top Rated Plus status
- Set realistic handling time — over-promising leads to negative feedback
- Account for weekends and holidays
- Add 1-day buffer to actual handling capability
- Upload tracking within the handling time window

## Insurance & Signature Confirmation

| Item Value | Recommendation |
|-----------|----------------|
| Under $50 | No insurance needed (eBay covers most claims) |
| $50 - $250 | Consider insurance, no signature required |
| $250 - $750 | Insurance recommended, signature optional but wise |
| Over $750 | Insurance REQUIRED, Signature Confirmation REQUIRED |

> **Critical**: For items over $750, eBay requires signature confirmation for seller protection. Without it, a buyer's "Item Not Received" claim will be decided in their favor.

## International Shipping

### Options

1. **eBay Global Shipping Program (GSP)**
   - Ship to eBay's US warehouse; they handle international logistics
   - Seller protected from international shipping issues
   - Easiest option but may be more expensive for buyers

2. **Direct International Shipping**
   - Lower cost for buyers, potentially more sales
   - Seller handles customs forms
   - Risk of international shipping disputes

3. **Exclude International**
   - Simplest but limits buyer pool
   - Consider for fragile/heavy/restricted items

### Customs Forms

- Accurately declare item value — falsifying customs values is illegal
- Include detailed item description
- Research restricted items by destination country
- Consider import duties impact on buyer experience

## Shipping Cost Reduction Tips

1. Buy shipping labels through eBay (discounted rates)
2. Use USPS free Priority Mail boxes when applicable
3. Consolidate multiple purchases for same buyer
4. Negotiate UPS/FedEx rates at high volume (100+ packages/month)
5. Use eBay's shipping supply program for free branded materials
6. Compare carriers for each shipment — no single carrier is always cheapest
