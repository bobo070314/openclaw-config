# Multi-Platform Seller Comparison & Decision Reference

## Overview

Help sellers choose where and how to sell by comparing fees, rules, risks, and strategies across eBay, Amazon, Walmart, FB Marketplace, Poshmark, and Mercari.

## Platform Fee Comparison

| Platform | Fee Structure | Typical Total Fee |
|----------|--------------|-------------------|
| Amazon | 8-45% referral (by category) + FBA fees + storage + return processing | 20-35% |
| eBay | 13.25% FVF (most categories) + $0.30/order + ~2.9% payment | 15-17% |
| Walmart | 8-15% referral (by category) | 8-15% |
| FB Marketplace | 5% selling fee (shipped items) | 5% |
| Poshmark | $2.95 (<$15) or 20% (>$15) | ~20% |
| Mercari | 10% selling fee + 2.9% + $0.50 payment | ~13% |

## True Cost Calculation

Never compare just platform fees. Calculate total cost:

```
True Cost = Platform Fee + Shipping + Payment Processing + Tax Obligations + Return Cost (rate × avg refund)
```

Return rates by category:
- Electronics: 15-20%
- Clothing/Shoes: 20-30%
- Home/Garden: 5-10%
- Books/Media: 2-5%

## Platform Selection Decision Matrix

| Factor | Amazon | eBay | Walmart | FB Marketplace |
|--------|--------|------|---------|----------------|
| Audience size | Largest | Large | Growing | Local/Large |
| Fee level | Highest | Medium | Lower | Lowest |
| Fulfillment | FBA available | Self-ship | WFS available | Self-ship/local |
| Returns risk | High | Medium | Medium | Low (local) |
| Brand control | Limited | Good | Limited | None |
| Auction option | No | Yes | No | No |

## Pricing Strategy

- **Only use sold/completed listings** for market value — active listing prices are meaningless
- Condition affects price significantly: "Good" vs "Very Good" can mean 30% difference
- Factor in platform-specific fees when setting prices across platforms
- Inventory and competition change hourly — always verify current market state

## Account Health & Risk

| Platform | Critical Metric | Danger Zone |
|----------|----------------|-------------|
| Amazon | ODR (Order Defect Rate) | >1% = suspension risk |
| eBay | Seller Performance Standards | Below Standard = restricted |
| Walmart | Seller Scorecard | Multiple warnings = removal |

### Suspension Triggers (All Platforms)

- Review manipulation → permanent ban
- IP/brand complaints → immediate suspension
- Selling counterfeit items → permanent ban + legal risk
- Excessive late shipments → account restrictions

## Cross-Platform Scam Patterns

| Scam Type | Platform | Red Flag |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| Triangle fraud | Local/eBay | "Ship to my friend, I'll pay extra" |
| Off-platform payment | All | "Pay me via Zelle/Venmo instead" |
| Fake stock photos | FB/Local | Only stock images, no real photos |
| Overpayment scam | eBay/Local | Pays more than asking, requests wire refund |
