# Cross-Border Ecommerce Tools Reference

## Overview

Comprehensive cross-border ecommerce toolkit covering multi-platform product research, keyword analysis, competitor intelligence, patent/IP risk detection, supplier sourcing, and market trends. Spans Amazon, TikTok Shop, eBay, Walmart, and 1688.

## Multi-Platform Product Research

### Supported Platforms

| Platform | Capabilities |
|----------|-------------|
| Amazon | Product search, ASIN details, BSR, reviews, A+ content, keyword reverse lookup |
| TikTok Shop | Trending products, new product rankings, shop analytics |
| eBay | Product listings, sold data, seller analysis |
| Walmart | Product search, pricing, availability |
| 1688 | Supplier search, MOQ, price tiers, supplier ratings |

### Product Research Workflow

1. Search by keyword, category, or image across target platforms
2. Extract key metrics: price, sales volume, review count, BSR ranking
3. Identify high-demand / low-competition opportunities
4. Cross-reference with 1688 for sourcing cost and margin calculation

## Keyword & Traffic Analysis

- **ASIN keyword reverse lookup** — Discover which keywords drive traffic to competing products
- **Traffic source breakdown** — Organic search vs PPC vs external traffic ratios
- **Keyword competition density** — Estimate difficulty of ranking for specific terms
- **Search volume trends** — Seasonal patterns and rising/falling keywords

## Competitor Deep Analysis

- Batch query ASIN details (price, sales estimates, review velocity)
- Analyze A+ Content quality and structure
- Mine reviews: extract pain points, feature requests, sentiment trends
- Track competitor pricing and inventory changes over time

## Patent & IP Risk Detection

Critical for cross-border sellers to avoid legal issues before listing.

| Check Type | What It Detects |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Design patent search | Visual similarity to registered designs |
| Invention patent search | Functional patent infringement risk |
| Trademark check | Text and graphic trademark conflicts |
| Copyright check | Content/image copyright issues |
| Policy compliance | Platform-specific restricted item rules |

### IP Check Workflow

1. Before listing any product, run patent and trademark checks
2. Provide product images and/or text descriptions for analysis
3. Review flagged risks and make go/no-go decision
4. Document clearance for compliance records

## 1688 Sourcing

- Search Chinese supplier catalogs by keyword or image
- Compare: MOQ, price tiers (1-99, 100-499, 500+), supplier ratings
- Check supplier years in business, transaction volume, response rate
- Calculate landed cost: product cost + shipping + customs + duties

## Market Trend Intelligence

- **Google Trends** — Real-time trending topics, keyword interest over time by region
- **Seasonal patterns** — Identify peak/off-season for product categories
- **Social media signals** — Viral products on TikTok, Instagram trending items

## Data Sources & Compliance

- Data obtained through official platform APIs and authorized third-party tools
- Respect each platform's Terms of Service and rate limits
- User is responsible for ensuring compliance with local import/export regulations
